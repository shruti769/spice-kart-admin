Privacy Policy

Spice Kart app · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Spicekart (ABN 67 699 205 939) collects through the Spice Kart app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address and date of birth, and your profile photo if you add one.
  • Sign-in details: if you sign in with your phone number, Google or Apple, we receive an identifier and the name and email that service shares with us.
  • Delivery details: the addresses you save, delivery notes, and the map location of each address.
  • Orders and payments: the items you order, totals, coupons, refunds and your Spice Kart Money balance. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Location: only if you allow it, your phone's location to fill in your address, check that we deliver to you, and record where an order was placed from.
  • Photos and camera: only if you choose to add a profile photo or attach a photo to a support request.
  • Support messages: what you send our team in the app, and any photos you attach.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.

How we use it

  • To take, deliver and support your orders, and to process payments and refunds.
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates by email, text message and push notification.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud.
  • To send offers and news, only if you turn on marketing notifications. You can turn them off at any time in the app.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share only what is needed with services that help us run the app:

  • Supabase stores our app data and account records.
  • Stripe processes card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments and refunds.
  • Google Firebase verifies phone numbers at sign-in. Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • Brevo sends our emails and text messages.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS) show the map when you add an address.

Some of these services store data outside Australia. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information, except order and payment records that we must keep for tax and accounting reasons.

Your choices

  • Get a copy of your data: your details, addresses and orders are shown in the app. Email us for a full copy.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Privacy & data → Delete account. You can also email us to ask.
  • Correct your details: edit them in the app at any time.
  • Location, camera, photos and notifications: turn these on or off in your phone's settings.

Children

Spice Kart accounts are for people aged 18 and over.

Contact us

For any privacy question or complaint, email Orders@spicekart.com.au or call 0430 827 427. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We will tell you in the app before we make important changes to this policy.
